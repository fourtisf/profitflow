FAQ

Questions, answered.

How is “realized profit” calculated?

We rebuild each wallet’s full on-chain history, derive its true average cost basis across every buy, and realize profit only when a sell clears. It’s the real cashed-out number — not a guess from a single transaction.

How accurate is it?

Figures are estimates from public on-chain data. Token transfers, airdrops and cross-wallet moves can distort cost basis, so we flag them instead of inventing a number. It’s an analytics tool, not financial advice.

Which chain and sources?

Solana. Swaps are parsed across pump.fun, Raydium and Meteora, with wash trades, MEV and liquidity moves filtered out so the realized figure reflects a genuine exit.

Do I need to sign up or connect a wallet?

No. The feed is read-only public data — no signup, no wallet connect to watch. Join the waitlist if you want the launch invite.

Is it free?

The public feed is free to watch. Deeper tools (wallet radar, sub-second alerts) are planned for a Pro tier — pricing isn’t finalized yet.
